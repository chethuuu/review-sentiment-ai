What I built - ReviewSense, a sentiment analyzer for reviews. You give it a review
(product, movie, whatever) and it tells you if it's positive or negative, with a
confidence %. Used TF-IDF + Logistic Regression instead of a transformer since it
trains fast, no GPU needed, and it's easy to actually explain what the model is
doing (which words pushed it toward positive/negative etc).

Trained on the NLTK movie_reviews corpus - 2000 real movie reviews (1000
positive / 1000 negative) from Pang & Lee's polarity dataset, a well known
public dataset for this kind of task. data/build_dataset.py downloads it and
writes it to data/reviews.csv. Test accuracy is ~81%, which is realistic for
this setup on real review text.
